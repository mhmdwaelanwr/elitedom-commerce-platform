# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project collaboration should be professional, technically rigorous, and respectful. Participants are expected to critique ideas and changes without attacking people, communicate constraints clearly, protect confidential information, and keep reviews focused on improving the system.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable review feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting factual errors;
- documenting decisions and trade-offs;
- respecting different levels of experience and areas of expertise;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, personal insults, or publication of private information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project chat used for repository work, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms contributors or project safety. Security-sensitive or privacy-sensitive material may be removed immediately from public discussion.

Concerns about conduct should be raised privately with the repository owner/maintainers through an available private project communication channel. Do not publish personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
