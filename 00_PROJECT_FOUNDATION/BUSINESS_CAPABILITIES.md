## 1. Introduction & Purpose
This document defines the core and supporting business capabilities required by the **Elitedom Store** platform to achieve its strategic vision, democratize hardware access, and maintain operational efficiency.

---

## 2. Core Business Capabilities
The solution supports the following primary business capabilities as outlined in the business architecture:

* **Customer Management:** Manages customer registration, authentication, profiles, addresses, and account dashboards.
* **Product Management:** Handles product catalogs, multi-level hierarchical categories, specifications, and hardware compatibility parameters.
* **Inventory Management:** Maintains real-time visibility and stock levels across physical warehouses and hybrid dropshipping suppliers.
* **Supplier Management:** Manages verified suppliers, vendor profiles, lead times, and performance metrics.
* **Procurement:** Handles automated purchase order (PO) generation and supplier routing when local safety stock thresholds are met.
* **Pricing Management:** Controls multi-currency exchange rate adjustments and B2B tiered pricelists.
* **Order Management:** Manages shopping carts, secure checkout sessions, order lifecycle transitions, and ERP synchronization.
* **Payment Management:** Supports secure online payment processing, digital wallets, Cash on Delivery (COD), and refund handling.
* **Shipping Management:** Coordinates fulfillment, delivery tracking, and courier integration for Egyptian governorates.
* **Warranty Management:** Provides item-level serial number tracking and automated warranty activation upon sale.
* **Customer Support:** Manages helpdesk ticketing, return merchandise authorization (RMA) claims, and technical consultations.
* **Loyalty Management:** Tracks reward points accumulation, customer retention tiers, and point redemptions at checkout.
* **Reporting & Analytics:** Generates executive dashboards, sales reports, inventory valuations, and financial summaries.
* **ERP Integration:** Maintains bi-directional synchronization middleware with Odoo ERP for seamless data flow.
* **User & Access Management:** Enforces Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), security permissions, and audit logging.
