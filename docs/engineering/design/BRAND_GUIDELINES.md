# Brand Guidelines

## Purpose

Defines engineering-facing brand consistency rules. Marketing/legal brand ownership may maintain richer external guidance.

## Reference

### Identity

Use Elitedom consistently as the product/platform name. Do not invent alternate marks/provider co-branding as primary identity.

### Tone

Customer copy should be clear, concise and trustworthy in both English and Arabic; avoid technical/provider jargon in checkout errors.

### Commerce trust

Prices, availability, payments, refunds, warranty and delivery copy must match server/provider state and avoid false guarantees.

### Localization

Arabic copy is a first-class experience with RTL layout; translation is not achieved by visually reversing English-only UI.

### Accessibility

Brand styling must not reduce contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operability or readable text sizing.
