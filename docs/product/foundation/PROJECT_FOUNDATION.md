# 1. Project Overview

Elitedom is an Egyptian technology retail and e-commerce startup focused on providing computer hardware, gaming accessories, peripherals, networking products, and technology solutions through a customer-first approach.

The business combines Stock-based sales and Dropshipping to maximize product availability while maintaining competitive pricing, operational flexibility, and exceptional customer service.

The long-term objective is to transform Elitedom from an online retailer into a trusted technology brand with physical retail stores, private-label products, and regional expansion.

---

# 2. Vision

To become one of Egypt's most trusted technology brands by delivering premium customer experience, transparent pricing, reliable after-sales service, and eventually building a private-label hardware and accessories brand manufactured through trusted global partners.

# 21. Business Operating Model

Elitedom operates using a hybrid business model combining Stock and Dropshipping.

Business Flow:

Supplier
→ Inventory / Dropshipping
→ Elitedom Platform
→ Customer Order
→ Payment
→ Order Processing
→ Shipping Partner
→ Customer
→ Warranty & After-Sales Service
→ Customer Loyalty

The operating model prioritizes customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and scalable growth.
